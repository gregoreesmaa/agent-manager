# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in agent-manager welcoming for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind, be direct, assume good intent.
- Respectful disagreement; no harassment, insults, or exclusionary jokes.
- Free software stays free: no paywall or tier proposals, ever.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainers (open an issue titled
"conduct report" or contact the release shepherd). Maintainers will review,
warn, and — for repeated or severe cases — ban. All decisions are final
and will be summarized publicly without naming reporters.
